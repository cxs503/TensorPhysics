## 3. Findings, severity-ranked

### F1 (blocking, claim-level) — the anchor's core deliverable, first-hand data, is not there
`feature_ledger` id 472 requires ">=2 个一手数据源入库（出处/单位/面积定义逐条记录）" and an
"ex 与环境参数（温度/应变率/晶粒尺寸）的关系表". The library has 7 records with the right *metadata
schema* (citation, units, area definition, geometry, conditions), but **0 of the 5 external records
are primary-verified** (`primary_source_verified=false` throughout
`data/ice_pressure_area/pressure_area_sources.json`), and the 2 records that are `true` are in-repo
transcriptions that the delivery itself forbids using as external truth. The
ex-vs-environment table does not exist: temperature/rate/grain columns are null in most records, and
the report says so ("withheld"). Criterion 1 and 2 of the anchor are therefore **unmet**; what was
delivered is a *literature meta-analysis with an area-convention algebra*, which the report also
states honestly. Consequence: the headline `ex = 0.450 [0.408, 0.492]` is not a first-hand
calibration and must not be quoted as one downstream.

### F2 (blocking, ledger) — no benchmark exists to certify L4
`tensor_benchmark_cases` has **0 rows** for `GAP-PP-2-ICE-STRUCT-PRESSURE-AREA-CALIB`. The 8
"reference vs computed" rows are, in substance:
* R1/R3/R4/R5 — self-consistency: a fit recovering the exponent that was declared for the same
  table, and invariance identities (these do verify the algebra; they do not benchmark it);
* R2 — a fit of the in-repo regression table against its own recorded value (drift check);
* R6/R7 — reproduction of the platform's own 1.32x/1.74x pair, at 1.99 % and **4.81 %**, against a
  per-row author tolerance of 5 %. At the ecosystem 3 % criterion R7 **fails**.
Nothing in the table compares a calibrated quantity against an independent measurement.

### F3 (blocking, integration) — the calibrated exponent is not wired into the platform consumer
`src/tensorphysics/ice_modules/local_pressure.py` (the live `LocalPressureModel`) still runs
`p = 14 · A^-0.15`. The delivery *detects* the contradiction (family conflict `iacs_ur_i` ex=0.45 vs
0.15, z=6.98, flagged "blocking") and *cross-checks* it, but does not resolve it and does not switch
the consumer. The gap between the two laws grows with area: 62.1 % at 5 m², 99.5 % at 10 m²,
162.6 % at 25 m², **223.3 % at 50 m²** (`calibration.json::consumer_crosscheck`). Only
`hull_icebelt_fem.calibrated_demand` consumes the calibration. So the anchor's "make the stress
bandwidth a quotable closed interval" is achieved **inside a new module**, not in the platform.

### F4 (major, reproducibility) — the only L4-grade evidence skips in the default test environment
`tests/conftest.py` pins `OPENBLAS/OMP/MKL_NUM_THREADS=1` and its comment asserts the pinning has
"performance only, no numerical effect". For the reused plate element that is false: I reproduced
σ_x = 18.254 MPa / w = 0.0545 mm pinned vs 91.764 MPa / 1.8896 mm unpinned (79.9 %/86.2 % error,
deterministic across repeats). The two FEM tests therefore `pytest.skip()` instead of asserting, so
"45 passed, 2 skipped" contains **no** FEM-vs-Navier evidence; the FEM numbers live only in a
hand-picked environment. The defect is honestly declared and a tripwire
(`all_reference_checks_pass`) exists — but the default test command cannot fail on it, and the
`conftest.py` comment is misleading for anyone relying on it.

### F5 (moderate, statistics) — the "5.2x bandwidth contraction" is assumption-driven, not new data
I reproduce the pooled interval exactly by inverse-variance pooling of the two *declared bands*
converted to σ at 90 % (k=2, τ=0): ex = 0.4500 [0.4084, 0.4916]. At the platform's area ratio
r = 2.3593 the demand bandwidth contracts 5.04x. Decomposition: the IACS record alone already gives
4.19x (its declared band [0.40, 0.50] is much narrower than the old 0.30–0.70 scan), and pooling the
second record adds only **1.20x ≈ 1/√2**. Moreover both pooled records are the same rule lineage
(ISO 19906 Annex A and IACS UR I patch pressure), so treating them as independent replicates
overstates the precision. The report presents the interval but not this decomposition; downstream
readers should not treat 5.2x as evidence strength.

### F6 (minor) — the measured-local stratum contradicts the rules stratum and is correctly kept
`measured_local` pools to 0.618 [0.495, 0.741] with I² = 57.9 %, i.e. above the declared band's top
end. The delivery reports this as a negative result rather than smoothing it away (credit), but it
also means a single exponent does not describe local pressures — the "quotable ex" only holds for the
rules stratum.

### F7 (minor) — unresolved blocking family conflicts are carried forward
Two blocking conflicts (IACS ex = 0.45 vs 0.15 vs 0.185) are detected and then left in place
("resolving them is a separate change"). Detection without disposition means the next consumer can
still pick the wrong number — as F3 shows.

## 4. What the delivery does get right (credit, verified independently)

* The area-convention algebra is correct. `r = (1 + t/2)/(1 + t)` for `A_d = A_c + λδ√A_c` is the
  exact log-derivative, and constant-ratio invariance is a theorem, not a fit; the delivered
  invariance rows are bit-identical (1.2e-14) and the analytic-vs-FD derivative agrees to 1e-9.
* The structural chain is real physics: my own Navier double-sine implementation (independent code,
  same panel 2.4×0.6×0.035 m AH36, same IACS patch 0.96×0.24 m, area 0.2304 m²) reproduces the
  delivery's reference σ_x to ≤ 1e-4 relative for all four exponents, and the reported pressure
  p(A) = 1.2·A^-ex also matches to 4 digits. The FEM converges monotonically 1.257 % → 0.389 %
  (mesh 24×6 → 40×10), well inside the 3 % acceptance tolerance and the 15 % platform limit.
* Reproducibility of the artefact set is excellent: 5/5 sha256 reproduced bit-identically from a
  clean copy.
* The report's §6 lists the thread defect, the absence of primary sources, the heterogeneity of the
  measured stratum and the unresolved conflicts — the honesty is above the ecosystem average.
