\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Agent harnesses for software engineering.}
SWE-agent~\cite{yang2024sweagent} established the executable
agent--computer interface for repository-level tasks; OpenHands~\cite{wang2024openhands}
(open formerly OpenDevin) generalizes this into an event-sourced agent
SDK with pluggable tools, and is the harness our daemon fleet embeds.
Commercial CLI agents (Claude Code, Codex CLI) demonstrated long-horizon
terminal-driven development but are single-tenant by design. Our work
does not propose a new harness; it contributes the \emph{multi-model,
multi-repository mission layer} above such a harness, with routing,
envelopes, and gates that the harnesses themselves do not provide.

\paragraph{Multi-agent LLM systems.}
AutoGen~\cite{wu2023autogen}, CAMEL~\cite{li2023camel}, and
LangGraph-style graph runtimes let developers script conversations among
LLM roles. MetaGPT~\cite{hong2024metagpt} encodes a software-team SOP
with role specialization. These systems orchestrate \emph{within} one
process graph; agents exchange messages, not auditable task objects with
budgets and acceptance chains. \sysname{} missions are durable rows with
claim tokens, heartbeats, and human-terminal acceptance, which is what
shared research infrastructure requires (crash recovery, cost control,
auditability), and our roles map to \emph{different backbone models}
rather than different prompts over one model.

\paragraph{LLM agents for science.}
Recent surveys~\cite{lu2024aiscientist,baek2024researchagent} catalog
autonomous hypothesis generation and tool use in natural sciences;
coscientist~\cite{boiko2023autonomous} drives wet-lab chemistry, and
ML-agent benchmarks evaluate ML engineering autonomy. Closest to us are
systems that couple LLM agents to numerical solvers or HPC
pipelines~\pending{cite specific 2025--2026 works at submission}. The
differentiator here is the \emph{co-development} setting: agents modify
the solver repositories themselves, under conservation gates, rather
than steering fixed solvers.

\paragraph{Coupled multiphysics integration.}
Partitioned multiphysics coupling (e.g., preCICE~\cite{bungartz2016precice})
standardizes field exchange, time stepping, and convergence acceleration
between separate solver codes. That literature defines \emph{what} must
hold at an interface (conservation, geometric and temporal consistency);
we adopt such identities directly as machine-checkable promotion gates
(S1 impulse/bond-energy ledgers, S2 conjugate force/work matching, S3
total-force conservation with temporal alignment, S4 clock
synchronization with sub-cycling, rollback, and checkpointing).

\paragraph{Pre-registration and auditability.}
Our append-only event stream and pre-registered per-stage criteria
follow the discipline of clinical preregistration adapted to
computational campaigns: hypotheses and machine criteria are committed
before execution, and outcomes---including honest failures---are
recorded immutably. This contrasts with post-hoc cherry-picking risks
in agent benchmarks and provides the raw data for \S\ref{sec:ablation}.
